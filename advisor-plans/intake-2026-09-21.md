## 6. Opinions

- The regex guard exists in one place and not the other. Either both user-regex sites need it or neither does. The only author of these patterns is the local user.
- `gui.py` is doing five jobs: window construction, settings load and save, the hotkey state machine, the pipeline worker, and delivery. B1, B2, B4, B6 and B7 all live there. Extracting the Tk-free parts (`_deliver`, the press/release/cancel logic) is what makes them testable. That is a refactor in service of tests, not tidiness.
- The glossary match type `"word"` is reported to behave exactly like `"phrase"`. Not confirmed.

## 8. Standards feedback

Proposed amendments to `standards.md`, with this run's evidence. The file was not edited.

1. **2.7 earned its keep. Keep "no numeric floor".** Total coverage is 51%. A 50% floor would have passed this repo. The hot-path wording caught `gui.py` at 0% and the untested clipboard restore.
2. **1.5 and 2.4 fire on the same line.** One `continue-on-error` produced a tier 1 blocker and a tier 2 deviation. Proposal: keep 1.5 as the rule, delete 2.4, and move "a job that cannot fail is removed or made blocking" into 1.5's text.
3. **2.15 is probably wrong as worded for this repo.** It generalises a ruling made for protonmail-bridge-docker. This repo is public, ships a `CLAUDE.MD`, and has 45 Claude trailers in history that only a rewrite would remove. Proposal: the rule becomes "the repo's CLAUDE.md states its attribution policy; the audit checks commits made after that statement against it". The default policy stays an open decision.
4. **2.1 needs a line on public repos.** Is a documented pip fallback for outside contributors allowed? If yes, the rule should say "uv is the documented primary path; a fallback is labelled as unsupported".
5. **2.8 cannot be checked as written.** "Newest supported version" has no meaning without an upper bound. Proposal: "the matrix covers the `requires-python` floor and the newest CPython the locked dependencies install on".
6. **2.14 says "on the homelab server".** The source memory does; the rule text says `~/git/`. Proposal: "in `~/git/` on whichever machine holds it".
7. **1.1 needs a named tool.** Neither scanner was installed, so a tier 1 rule got a grep. Proposal: name one. `uvx detect-secrets scan` fits the uv rule and needs no install; `gitleaks` scans history better.
8. **Missing rule: statements the product makes to its user about privacy or data handling must be true.** B5 is a false privacy claim in the UI. 2.12 covers docs, not UI text. Proposal: widen 2.12 to "docs and user-facing claims match reality", or add it to tier 1 for anything about what is stored or sent.
9. **Missing rule: no tracked leftovers at the repo root.** `commit.txt` has no rule to fail. Low value; tier 3 at most.
10. **Skill feedback.** The "if a skill is not installed, skip it" clause was too strict here: the skill files were readable over the WSL share and following them worked. Proposal: "if the skill cannot be invoked but its files can be read, follow them and say so".
